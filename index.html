04 / Working approach

A method, not a black box.

University gives me the foundations; personal projects give me room to make decisions, investigate problems, and learn from the results. This is the approach I aim to bring to collaborations.

01 / DEFINE

Start with a clear scope

Agree on the task, interfaces, and expected outcome. Be open about what I already know and what needs investigation.

02 / CHECK

Make behavior observable

Use tests, simulation, logs, or measurements appropriate to the task. Distinguish a simulated result from a hardware measurement.

03 / EXPLAIN

Leave a useful handover

Document the build steps, decisions, and limitations so that the next person can understand and continue the work.
